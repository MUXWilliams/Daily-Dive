## 4. Daily monitoring priorities

Look for developments in the following order of editorial importance.

1. **Ownership and consolidation**
   - Acquisitions, divestitures, private-equity investments, mergers, recapitalizations, or bankruptcy proceedings
   - A brand moving into or out of a parent-company portfolio
   - Newly created subsidiaries or holding companies

2. **Leadership and organizational changes**
   - New CEO, president, managing director, chairman, or founder transition
   - Major layoffs, facility closures, restructurings, or regional expansions

3. **Distribution and channel changes**
   - Exclusive distributor appointments or terminations
   - Brands bringing distribution in-house
   - New direct-to-consumer, wholesale, or regional operations
   - Clearly label distribution relationships as distribution, not ownership

4. **Product and technology news**
   - Meaningful pump, controller, lighting, filtration, or automation launches
   - New interoperability, cloud-control, API, app, or ecosystem integrations
   - Patents, licensing agreements, and major R&D announcements

5. **Safety, quality, and regulatory developments**
   - Product recalls, electrical or fire-safety notices, warranty changes, or regulator actions
   - Material product defects or service campaigns confirmed by an official source

6. **Manufacturing and supply chain**
   - New factories, capacity expansions, manufacturing relocation, tariffs, import restrictions, or major shortages
   - Significant changes to country of manufacture or component sourcing

7. **Financial developments**
   - Iwaki earnings, guidance, large-shareholder filings, or material stock-exchange disclosures
   - Credible reports of financing or financial distress at private manufacturers

8. **Industry events and recognition**
   - Major trade-show launches, strategically important partnerships, or credible industry awards
   - Avoid filling the newsletter with routine promotional posts

## 6. Source and verification policy

Use this source hierarchy:

1. Official company newsroom, investor-relations page, legal notice, regulatory filing, or government registry
2. Official statement from an acquiring company, parent company, authorized distributor, or executive
3. Established aquarium-industry or pet-industry trade publication
4. Reputable general business or financial publication
5. Retailer, forum, social-media post, or video only as a lead requiring further verification

For every included news item:

- Confirm the event is genuinely new, not an old announcement being reposted.
- Record both the article's publication date and the event's effective date when they differ.
- Link to the most authoritative available source.
- If ownership is central to the story, verify it against at least one official corporate source.
- Use two independent sources for acquisitions, recalls, litigation, or financial-distress reports when no primary source is available.
- Clearly label analysis or inference. Do not present inference as an announced fact.
- Never infer ownership merely because one company distributes, sells, services, or integrates another company's products.

### Ownership language

Use precise verbs:

- **Owns / acquired:** legal or controlling ownership is confirmed.
- **Parent company:** the brand or subsidiary sits within the parent's corporate structure.
- **Portfolio company:** a private-equity sponsor publicly lists the business as a current investment.
- **Distributes / exclusive distributor:** commercial distribution only; not ownership.
- **Manufactures for / OEM:** manufacturing relationship only; not necessarily ownership.
- **Partners with / integrates with:** commercial or technical relationship only.

## 7. Inclusion and exclusion rules

### Include

- Material corporate, ownership, leadership, distribution, product, safety, manufacturing, financial, or regulatory news
- News that affects product availability, compatibility, warranty coverage, service, pricing, or competitive dynamics
- Significant activity involving the companies and brands in the canonical map
- Closely adjacent developments that directly affect aquarium pumps, flow systems, controllers, filtration, or reef-equipment retail

### Exclude or deprioritize

- Routine sales, coupon codes, giveaways, affiliate promotions, or restock notices
- Ordinary social posts showing customer aquariums
- Unverified forum rumors or retailer speculation
- Minor firmware changes without meaningful customer impact
- Generic product roundups that do not contain new information
- Repeated coverage of the same announcement without a substantive update

## 9. Known uncertainties and cautions

- Private companies generally do not publish complete shareholder lists. Do not invent ownership percentages.
- A managing director, president, chairman, or legal representative is not automatically the sole owner.
- Jebao and Jecod are commonly used interchangeably; explain the shared manufacturer when relevant.
- Aperture's prior North American distribution of Maxspect did not constitute ownership. Maxspect established Maxspect North America LLC in December 2025 and brought North American operations in-house.
- CoralVue's distribution of Abyzz does not constitute ownership.
- TUNZE USA LLC and regional SICCE entities are market subsidiaries/operations; they do not supersede the global brand owners.
- Red Dragon, Bubble King, Dreambox, Hydro Wizard, Hydro Tube, and Panta Surge are product brands, not independent parent companies.
- Iwaki is publicly traded. Describe it as shareholder-owned rather than assigning it to one individual unless a current filing confirms control.
- Pan World was created by pump-industry engineers and competes closely with Iwaki, but it is not an Iwaki subsidiary.
- ReeFlo has historical associations with MDM/Sequence products. Confirm the current legal entity and trademark owner before describing any present-day corporate relationship.
